ZigSolver

This installs the ZigSolver desktop app and the solver it runs. The interface lives inside the app; the solver runs beside it on your own machine and is reachable only from the app.

Two things go on this Mac:

  • ZigSolver.app in /Applications
  • the solver, its models and its caches in /Library/Application Support/ZigSolver

An internet connection is required. After the files are copied, the installer sets up Homebrew, Python and the Python packages the solver needs — including PyTorch, which is a few hundred megabytes. On a machine that has none of these yet, expect the last step to take a while and to look like it has stalled. It has not.

You will be asked for your password: the app is installed for everyone on this Mac.
